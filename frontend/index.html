Lighthouse 8-Puzzle

The great lighthouse of Alexandria has crumbled into nine tiles. Pick a search algorithm and watch it slide the pieces back into place. When the tower stands whole, its lamp lights up the night. The empty slot rests in the top-left corner of the finished board.

Mode

Your game

Click two tiles on the board to swap them, or type a board above. Only boards with an even inversion count can be solved.

Click a tile next to the blank, or use the arrow keys (the tile slides the way you press). Z undoes. The Step slider and Prev/Next rewind your game; make a move from an earlier step to branch. Par is the optimal move count, found by A*.
